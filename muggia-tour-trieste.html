Muggia

Across the bay from Trieste lies Muggia, Italy’s little corner of Istria. Pastel houses, a quiet harbour and narrow lanes carry the memory of centuries of Venetian rule.

It is where the tour slows down: a walk through the historic centre, a view from above, and time to eat by the water at your own pace.

What to expect

  • Time for lunch (not included in the price)
  • Walk through the old town with a local guide
  • Short drive from Trieste, far from the crowds
