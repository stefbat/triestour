A city that lives between the sea and the wind

Trieste does not shout (the Bora sometimes does). It waits at the end of Italy, where the Karst falls into the Adriatic, and rewards those who slow down: a castle white against the water, a cozy fishing village that cuddles you, hills hovering over the gulf.

We drive you there, gently, in a small group, so you can simply look. Leave the car, the map and the hurry to us.

Sunset specialty

Trieste is a panoramic jewel. Seen from above, the whole city and the gulf unfold at once: rooftops, ships drifting like slow thoughts, the Adriatic shining in the evening light.

Along the way we stop at various viewpoints, chosen for the light, to watch the gulf from the best seats in town.

One day, three moods

Our objective is to have the best panoramas. We meet 8 hours before the sun sets, so the day ends in the best light possible.

What guests say

Good to know

Do we really see the sunset?

Yes. We time our final stop at Grad Socerb for sunset. The tour runs in rain as long as it’s safe, though clouds may hide the sunset. The views over the Gulf are worth the trip even in the rain. Pick a date on the home page to see the exact time.

What is included?

Minivan transport, a Triestinian guide, a local snack and all stops. Lunch and optional tickets (for example the Miramare castle interior) are not included.

What languages does the guide speak?

Your local guide is happy to chat with you along the way. He’s a native Italian speaker, speaks fluent English, and can hold simple conversations in German.

How do I pay?

Send a request on the contact page or on WhatsApp. You receive a secure payment link, and your seats are confirmed once paid.

Can I cancel?

Yes, with a full refund up to 48 hours before the tour. If we cancel for weather or safety, you get a refund or a free new date.

Do I need my passport?

Grad Socerb is just across the border in Slovenia. Please bring an ID card or passport.

Where do we meet?

In Piazza Unità d'Italia, Trieste. We bring you back to the same place.
